Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who We Are

Tempera is a mobile action game published by Vichovis.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your information is handled, please contact us:

  • Email: katsumi.mizutanip@gmail.com
  • Website: https://vichovis.github.io/

Information We Collect

Tempera does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other personal information directly to us.

We do not operate our own server or user database for storing personal information.

However, Tempera uses third-party services for advertising and in-app purchases. These services may independently collect or process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

Depending on the platform, device, settings, and applicable laws, this information may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and app version
  • IP address
  • General location derived from IP address
  • Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers
  • Advertising interactions, such as ad impressions, views, clicks, or completed rewarded ads
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information related to in-app purchases and transactions

The exact information processed depends on the services available on your device and your privacy settings.

Advertising

Tempera may display two types of advertisements:

  • Interstitial advertisements, which may appear during gameplay or progression.
  • Rewarded advertisements, which users may voluntarily watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

Tempera uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

Advertising services may process device and advertising-related information to deliver, measure, personalize, and improve advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure advertising performance.

Google AdMob

Tempera uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for advertising.

Google may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and advertising interaction information for purposes related to advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

For information about data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, please visit:

https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

In-App Purchases

Tempera includes an optional non-consumable in-app purchase called Remove Ads.

On iOS, purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and Apple's in-app purchase services.

We do not receive or store users' credit card numbers, payment credentials, or other payment information used to complete purchases.

Apple processes payment and transaction information according to its own privacy policy.

Remove Ads

When a user purchases or restores Remove Ads, interstitial advertisements are disabled.

Rewarded advertisements remain available as optional advertisements even after purchasing Remove Ads.

Apple App Store and In-App Purchases

For iOS users, Apple processes purchases and related transaction information through the App Store and Apple's in-app purchase system.

Apple may process information associated with a transaction, such as purchase history, transaction identifiers, and information necessary to complete and restore purchases.

For more information, please review Apple's Privacy Policy:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Locally Stored Game Data

Tempera may store game progress and gameplay-related settings locally on the user's device.

This may include:

  • Level or chapter progress
  • Game completion status
  • Local purchase status used by the game
  • Gameplay settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on the device and is not intentionally transmitted to servers operated by us.

Removing the app may remove locally stored game data, depending on the operating system and device configuration.

Purchase information may also be restored through Apple's App Store purchase system.

Data Retention

We do not operate our own server database containing users' personal information.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the respective provider's policies and applicable legal requirements.

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, removed with the app, or otherwise cleared by the operating system.

Purchase information may remain available through Apple's services so that purchases can be verified or restored.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties for purposes unrelated to operating Tempera.

Third-party services integrated into Tempera, such as Google AdMob and Apple's App Store services, may independently process information as necessary to provide advertising, payment processing, purchase verification, fraud prevention, and related services.

These providers are responsible for their own processing of information under their respective privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Tempera does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to us.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because Tempera uses third-party services such as Google AdMob and Apple services, those services may have their own age restrictions, privacy practices, and requirements.

Parents and guardians should review the relevant policies of these providers where applicable.

If you believe that we have received personal information from a child and would like us to review or delete it, please contact us using the information below.

Your Choices

Depending on your device, operating system, region, and applicable laws, you may have options to manage advertising privacy settings or limit personalized advertising through your device or platform settings.

You can also choose not to watch rewarded advertisements.

Purchasing Remove Ads disables interstitial advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements remain optional and available after purchasing Remove Ads.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by third-party services, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Because third-party providers such as Apple and Google may process information directly, requests concerning information held by those providers may need to be made directly to the relevant provider.

If you believe that information has been provided directly to us and would like to request access, correction, or deletion, please contact us.

Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid collecting or storing personal information that is not necessary to operate Tempera.

Information transmitted to third-party services is handled according to the security practices of those providers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Tempera may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Apple App Store / In-App Purchases — iOS purchases and transaction processing

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Tempera, the services used by the game, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the Last updated date at the top of this page.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
